How B2B Market works

One marketplace for both sides of the deal, with fewer steps between a need and a confirmed order.

For buyers
  1. {{ic:search ic-s}}Search or post a request. Find products by name, category or supplier. Can’t find it? Describe what you need once.
  2. {{ic:scale ic-s}}Compare quotes side by side. Matching suppliers reply with price, delivery and terms.
  3. {{ic:shield ic-s}}Order from a reviewed supplier. Negotiate directly. Nothing is ordered until you confirm.
  4. {{ic:list ic-s}}Track every request and order. It all stays in one dashboard.

Requests for quote, explained

Post one request for any product or quantity. Include the delivery city and timing, and matching suppliers reply with pricing. You choose who to work with, and nothing is ordered until you confirm.

  • {{ic:check ic-s}}Each request accepts up to ten quotes, so replies stay readable.
  • {{ic:check ic-s}}Your company name stays private until a supplier quotes.
  • {{ic:check ic-s}}We flag quotes that miss a delivery cost or a specification.

How verification works

Suppliers are reviewed for business registration and quality standards before they can list a product. Each storefront shows a checklist, and a line reads “Checked” only when our team has seen the document.

Reply time and answer rate are measured by the platform from real quotes. Reviews come only from buyers with a completed order.
